Fasting Shift Android privacy policy

This policy applies to the Fasting Shift Android app from the developer theonngroup. It covers optional partner weeks in supported Android builds and the app waitlist on this website. For privacy questions and app support, email onnifyworks@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

You can use Fasting Shift without an account. Its timer, planner and history use an on-device database. The app encrypts that database and uses Android Keystore to protect the key that unlocks it. The app keeps preferences, including reminder settings and your selected city, in private app storage.

  • Fast times, goals, fasting windows, schedules, weekly plan changes, notes and optional reflections power your timer, history and personal reviews.
  • Screening answers about age, height, weight, pregnancy or breastfeeding, eating-disorder history, diabetes and medication determine which fasting options and guidance appear.
  • Optional entries about symptoms, mood, weight, water, electrolytes, glucose or ketones power your logs, check-ins and trends.
  • Optional Ramadan, Sunnah and qada records, fast types, your selected city and prayer method support observance calendars and reminders. These records can reveal religious practice. You choose a city manually. The app uses bundled data and local calculations for prayer times.
  • Guided-program progress, local challenge selections and progress on a plan shared by link are recorded on your phone. A challenge invitation includes the settings you choose to share. People who join do not gain access to your personal fasting or health history.

Fasting Shift does not sell your health or religious-observance information or send these records to an advertising, analytics or billing service. Reminders and widgets can make fasting information visible on a lock screen or home screen. You control these displays in the app and Android settings.

Optional partner weeks

In Android builds that support partner weeks, you can invite one person for a week of check-ins or encouragement. The invitation page passes the private code to your Android app when you choose to open it. The website keeps the code in the link fragment. Opening the page does not redeem the invitation. Share the link only with the intended recipient.

When you create or accept a partner invitation, Fasting Shift uses Supabase to store your chosen display name, role, time zone for scheduling, a random pair identifier, membership and week dates, days checked in, preset encouragement and your choice about another week. A random credential on each phone allows that device to use the shared week. The service stores a hashed credential. Joining requires no email address or public profile.

Your partner sees your display name, role, shared week, dates you checked in, preset encouragement and your renewal choice. Sharing a check-in works on rest days, too. Fasting times, weight, hunger logs, reasons for checking in, screening answers and religious records stay outside the partner service. Partner activity is excluded from advertising.

Your planned Partner Week check-in days, reminder time and quiet hours stay on your phone. Your partner sees the days you actually check in. If you request another week, the service shares your request with your partner. A new week starts when both people agree. In versions with the Pause here option, you can withdraw a pending request before the new week starts. Once the service confirms the withdrawal, your partner’s next refresh shows that the request is gone.

To remove the shared week, choose Leave in Partner week while connected. Once the service confirms you have left, it erases the shared pair and its activity for both people. Uninstalling or clearing app data on its own removes access on your device. If you want to erase the shared week, leave before uninstalling. A new week clears the previous week's activity. Pending invitations expire after seven days. An unrenewed week expires 30 days after it ends. Expired invitations and weeks become inaccessible immediately. An hourly cleanup deletes their records, including both partners’ check-ins and encouragement.

In builds with Show QR, the app draws the same private invitation link as a QR code on your phone. Anyone who scans or photographs it can obtain the invitation link. You choose whom to show it to.

Calendar copies

In builds with Add to calendar, choosing that action opens your calendar editor with a neutral event title and the start and finish times. You choose the calendar and whether to save. Your calendar provider manages its copy, including any calendar sharing or sync you have enabled. Later edits in Fasting Shift do not update that copy.

Optional packaged-food lookup

In builds with food label lookup, you can enter a product barcode or scan one with Google Play services. Scanning takes place on the device. When you choose to look up the product, Fasting Shift sends the barcode to Open Food Facts. Like other internet services, Open Food Facts receives your IP address and an application identifier in the request. Fasting Shift leaves your fasting history, Health Connect records and partner details out of that request. Product information comes from the Open Food Facts database. See its privacy policy.

Exports, backups and sharing

Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for the app's private data. To move records, use the app’s backup and restore controls. An export of fasting history produces a CSV with finished fasts, including dates, times, protocol and fast type. A full JSON backup or ZIP export can include health logs, screening answers, notes, religious records and settings.

Exported files are unencrypted. Anyone with access to a file can read it. Android’s file picker lets you select the destination, including a cloud drive. The provider you select handles its copy under its privacy policy. When importing a history CSV, you can review the proposed records before adding them. Restoring a full backup replaces the local database records and applies the settings included in the file.

If you share an invitation, a progress card, a PDF report or another file, the receiving app and recipients can access what you send. You choose where it goes using Android’s sharing controls.

Optional Health Connect access

In builds with Walking this week, you can separately allow Fasting Shift to read step counts from Health Connect for your weekly recap. Your partner cannot see these counts. You can revoke step access in Health Connect.

If you turn on sync and grant permission, Fasting Shift can read recent weight entries into your weight log and write the weight and water entries you record to Health Connect. A separate permission lets it write glucose entries you record. It does not read glucose or write ketones or religious records to Health Connect.

If you open Body signals in Shift+ and grant access, the app can read sleep sessions and resting heart rate to compare fasting and non-fasting days. These values are read for that screen and are not saved in the app’s database. Fasting Shift uses Health Connect data for the features you enable in accordance with Google’s Health Connect Limited Use requirements.

If a measurement fails to sync, the app keeps the measurement locally and stores its local row number and fingerprint in private preferences for a retry. Retry references are excluded from export and backup files. You can disable sync or revoke individual permissions in Health Connect. Revoking access stops future access. To remove existing copies, use the deletion controls in the apps and in Health Connect. Other apps you authorized may hold their own copies.

Ads on the free plan

The free plan can show a Google AdMob banner in Settings. Shift+ is ad-free. Fasting Shift requests non-personalized ads. Your fasting history, screening answers, manually selected city, religious practices and Health Connect records are never sent to advertising providers or used to select or measure ads.

Google may use your IP address to estimate your general location. Its advertising and consent services can process device or app identifiers, privacy choices, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. The app does not request access to the Android advertising ID.

Where privacy choices are available, open Settings → Privacy and data → Ad privacy choices to review or change them. See how Google uses information from apps and how Google retains data.

Optional Shift+ purchases

Google Play processes purchases and code redemptions. When billing is enabled in your build, RevenueCat starts when you open the app and uses a generated app user ID to check access, including before you buy. It processes available purchase history, products, subscription status, and billing-related app and device information to grant or restore access and report subscription activity. It can determine a country from store information or the network address. Fasting Shift does not send RevenueCat your fasting, screening, measurement or religious records. Read the RevenueCat privacy policy and Google privacy policy for their handling of data.

Website waitlist

If you join the app waitlist, we store your email address, signup time and the updates you requested in Supabase. We use this list for Android launch news and group-fast invitations. You can join without giving us any health information. To leave the list or request deletion of your address, email onnifyworks@gmail.com.

Retention, deletion and support

Local records remain on your phone until you remove them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To erase local records, settings and pending sync references inside Fasting Shift, go to Settings → Privacy and data → How your data is protected → Delete all data and confirm. Separately delete any exports, backups or shared copies from the destinations where you saved or sent them.

Google Play holds its own transaction records. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Uninstalling Fasting Shift does not cancel a subscription. Contact us about billing information held on our behalf, including a request for deletion. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, and use them to handle your request. You do not need to send your screening answers or export your health records to ask for help. Email onnifyworks@gmail.com.

Fasting Shift is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Its screening restricts access to fasting plans for users under 18.

# Run the Fasting Shift Android pilot

Use the [task scorecard](pilot-tasks.csv) for observed tasks. Use the [offer and follow-up scorecard](pilot-follow-up.csv) for the price question and, with separate opt-in, the two-week pilot. Both files have five blank participant slots. Keep the templates in Git and completed copies in the research team's private workspace. Record anonymous IDs and product observations. Keep names, contact details, screenshots of personal screens, receipts, screening answers, fasting logs, and health readings out of the scorecards.

For onboarding, Today, partner privacy and the day-seven follow-up, use the [first-week pilot](first-week-pilot.md).

For the separate 20-pair Partner Week pilot, use the [two-week protocol and pair scorecard](partner-week-pilot.md).

## Set up one demo before the first session

Use a dedicated test phone or emulator with fictional records. Set up two upcoming weekday starts at 8 pm and two completed same-month demo records of equal duration. Note the dates and expected total. Start in light appearance with no active timer. Reset the fictional fixture between participants. Use the same build throughout the round and enter its version or commit in the task sheet.

One repeatable fixture uses Monday 23 November 2026 at 7 pm in Singapore, a 14-hour window, and 8 pm starts on Tuesday 24 and Wednesday 25. The completed records are 8 am–noon on Saturday 21 and Sunday 22. Correcting Sunday's end to 1 pm should bring the total to nine hours. On a physical phone, substitute dates that are available on its current calendar and check the answer key beforehand.

## Run a 20-minute session

Say “We’re testing the app. Please show me what you’d do in the example. You can skip a task or stop whenever you like.” Keep the answer column to yourself. Start each task from Timer, including the search for the paid example. Leave the timer idle after the preview task so the history fixture stays comparable.

| Task | Read aloud, substituting the fixture dates | What counts as success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 · Preview a finish | “This example starts a 14-hour window now. What time would it finish? What if it had started at 11 pm yesterday?” | Gives the local date and time in each case. In the fixed fixture the finishes are Tuesday at 9 am and Monday at 1 pm. |
| T2 · Change one evening | “Dinner on Tuesday is now at 9:30 pm. The rest of the week should stay as planned. Make that change and tell me what happens on Wednesday.” After they answer, ask “Dinner is back at 8 pm. Can you put Tuesday back?” | Tuesday alone moves to 9:30 pm, Wednesday stays at 8 pm, and restoring Tuesday removes the exception. |
| T3 · Correct a record | “Sunday’s entry ended at 1 pm. Please correct it and tell me the total fasting time shown for this month.” | Only Sunday's entry changes. The person reads hours for the month and identifies the expected total. Record a wrong-record edit even if they later recover. |
| T4 · Daily controls | “Add a 250 ml glass of water, switch to dark appearance, and return to the timer.” | One portion is added, dark appearance takes effect, and Timer is reachable again. |
| T5 · Explain the paid example | “Find an example of what Shift+ would give you. Whose records are these? What, if anything, stands out? Show me what makes you say that.” | Finds the sample, identifies the records as fictional and opens the dated evidence for the recurring weekday unaided. The current sample shows Tuesday 9 June at 9 pm and Tuesday 23 June at 9:30 pm. Choosing to keep the routine is a valid decision. |

Record the first wrong tap or confusing label and the first hint, if any. Use **unaided**, **assisted**, **incomplete** or **skipped** for the outcome. An unstarted task stays blank until the session closes. Flag **wrong-record**, **checkout**, or **accessibility** when applicable. Capture Android version, language, text scale, theme and TalkBack in the setup column once per person. Keep a repeat with large text or TalkBack as a separate row labeled “repeat” so it cannot inflate first-attempt completion.

After T5, ask “What decision, if any, would this report help you make?” Then show the complete intended price, currency and billing interval. Record whether it is a live offer or a labeled price concept. Ask “At that price, would you choose Shift+, keep the free app, or decide later? What led to that choice?” Record **paid**, **free**, **undecided** or **declined**. If no price is available, record **not shown** and leave price-specific intent unscored.

## Follow up once the full 14 days have elapsed

Enrollment in the follow-up is voluntary and separate from the observed session. Enter the opt-in timestamp with its timezone offset. Blank enrollment rows do not count. Leave ordinary use unprompted during week two. At follow-up, give the participant their week-two dates, then ask “During that period, which parts of the app did you use, if any? What brought you back?” If needed, ask specifically about looking at History or changing a plan. A verbal answer or an optional diary summary is enough.

- Days 8–14 run from seven days after enrollment up to, and excluding, fourteen days after enrollment. Enter **history**, **plan**, **both**, **neither**, or **unknown** for that interval. Opening Timer alone is **neither**. Use **unknown** for no response, an uncertain answer, or withdrawal from further contact. Leave a participant whose window is still open as **pending**.
- At a stated reporting cutoff, count all enrollees whose full 14 days elapsed, including unknown outcomes. Divide the number reporting **history**, **plan**, or **both** by that denominator. Report the counts of **neither**, **unknown** and **pending** alongside it. Describe it as self-reported return use. For example, two returners, one confirmed neither, one unknown and one pending gives **2/4** eligible, with one pending.
- After four **finished** weeks with plan history, willing participants may describe one decision their personal review helped them make. Record **changed**, **kept**, **no decision**, **declined**, or **not yet eligible**. They can also try the optional [two-phone friend session](friends-beta.md).
- Report stated price preference by the offer actually shown. Leave purchases and renewals **unmeasured** until a commercial offer is available. At launch use aggregate billing records for real purchases and renewals with the relevant offer, eligible cohort, observation window and data source. License-test transactions belong in billing QA. Never infer a purchase from the interview response.

Summarize first-attempt task outcomes as counts, the first point of friction and one fix to retest. Use the [roadmap decision table](product-roadmap.md#how-pilot-results-change-the-backlog) to choose the next change. Report pricing and return-use results as exploratory counts for this small cohort.

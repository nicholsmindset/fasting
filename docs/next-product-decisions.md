# Fasting Shift after the first-week release

Decision record for Robert Nichols · 28 November 2026

**Product promise.** Help people who already have a routine adjust when their week changes. Supporters can show up without fasting themselves. Ramadan, Sunnah and qada are optional paths people can choose. The free timer, history, safety tools, basic Partner Week and core Ramadan tracking stay accessible. The paid hypothesis is that people will return for guidance they recognize as Robert’s work.

## Three next experiments

| Order | First delivery | Evidence to continue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Make the recap actionable | After “Partly” or “No”, offer “Adjust next week”. Show next week’s dates and let the person move a start, choose a rest day, or keep the week. Show a before/after preview. Apply only after confirmation. Keep active timers, prior records and unrelated reminders intact. Reuse My Week overrides. | Five-person scenario: ask each person to move one night without changing another. At least four complete it unaided. Software checks cover time zones, overnight shifts, repeat taps and undo. In an opt-in pilot track accepted edits and returns the following week with raw counts. |
| 2. Publish a small, useful book library | Start with three free readings such as “Dinner ran late”, “I stopped early” and “Reading my history”. Give each a source page, one action and an optional reflection. Offer an optional 60–90 second recording by Robert with a transcript. Keep the paid 21-day path separate, with approved English and reviewed Malay/Indonesian before publishing. | Readers can find a relevant lesson from Today or History in two choices. Test offline access, large text and audio-off use. Measure voluntary second reads and return to a second lesson. Check that a reader can say what Shift+ adds. |
| 3. Give visitors a useful result before asking them to install | Pilot one page for a changing work schedule. Let the visitor enter a usual finish time and preview two editable local start/finish times. No email needed to see the result. Put an explicitly optional book or group waitlist after it. For Ramadan, start with a single useful Singapore page with source, locality, validity dates and offline behavior. | Verify midnight/date changes and authority data against published dates. Measure anonymous page-to-Play clicks separately from confirmed installs. Expand to new cities only after source coverage, original local usefulness and demand justify the work. |

One-tap logging is a usability check on the existing actions. Time the flow to record a water entry, to log how someone feels and to undo a mistake. Improve the slowest measured step. Keep the supporter pilot ahead of any larger family or workplace circle. Exclude mosque groups in line with the existing product decision.

## Pricing, faith features and deliberate deferrals

* Use factual store language. “No account needed for the timer”, “Your full fasting history is free” and “Manage your subscription in Google Play” can each be tested. Explain which information is transmitted when a user joins Partner Week. For renewals, show the actual amount, date when known, interval and management link. Explain how the user can enable reminders. Notification delivery can be disabled.
* Keep the proposed US$59.99 annual price, 21-day trial, and S$9.90 Ramadan Pass as hypotheses. First verify Google Play purchase, restore, cancellation and expiry with licensed test accounts. A finite book course can suit a one-time offer. An annual subscription needs continuing value, such as recurring authored releases or cohorts with actual delivery. Compare trial lengths on retained paid users and net revenue per eligible visitor after equal observation, alongside refund and billing complaint rates. The RevenueCat benchmark reports medians across cohorts and does not establish Fasting Shift’s optimum. [Play subscriptions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533) · [RevenueCat report](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/)
* For a future Ramadan Pass, test demand for additional authored seasonal content. Keep prayer times, qada and safety free. Any giving entry should be voluntary and separate from a missed check-in. In Singapore show the current official guidance and designated channels. A qada count alone cannot establish a person’s fidyah. Keep donation penalties, automatic charges, religious scoring and commercial sponsorship out of the plan. [MUIS guidance](https://www.muis.gov.sg/resources/media-releases/muis-removes-compounded-fidyah-requirement-and-announces-zakat-fitrah-rates-for-ramadan-2026-1447h/)
* Use Play Billing for the Android offer. A useful website can generate interest independently. In-app links to alternative checkout depend on the market and program enrollment. The US external links program does not establish eligibility in Singapore, Malaysia or Indonesia. [Google Play US program](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16470497)
* Revisit clinician-led medication content, a clinic pilot, sponsored listings, branded supplements and major scanner changes as separate decisions after evidence of demand, ownership and delivery capacity. Treat proposed January dates as planning targets dependent on Play release access, content approvals and locally verified timetables. Do not ask for medication use, weight, fasting logs or religious practice for advertising or marketing attribution.

Review the first-week cohort results alongside [the pilot protocol](first-week-pilot.md). Select the next implementation from observed task failures and repeat use. Revisit pricing after the approved program is available to test.

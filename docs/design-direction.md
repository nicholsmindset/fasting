# Fasting Shift design direction

The most distinctive thing Fasting Shift can show is what happens to a real week when dinner runs late. Place the clock, the affected date and the revised schedule ahead of routine logging actions. The visual identity already has a useful foundation in the Nichols mark, warm light surface, near-black dark surface, emerald accent and tabular time display.

## What the native screens reveal

The comparison used the November Android captures of the ready timer, active timer, populated History, empty Log, Settings and Reminders in both themes. The separate weekly-review captures add a personal report, a sample report and a later large-text timer. The table distinguishes what is visible in those captures from behavior traced in the Compose source. The final column describes an acceptance task for the redesign. Participant results will come from the pilot.

## How controls should read

- **Primary action.** Reserve the filled emerald button on Timer for starting or ending the selected fast. Keep the time, target and action on one visual axis. Use quieter controls for changing the view and correcting times.
- **Navigation and choices.** A trailing forward chevron opens a destination bearing the same name. A downward indicator beside the current value opens a short choice sheet. Mark the current option visibly, restore focus to its trigger on dismissal and keep longer tasks on their own pages.
- **Immediate preferences.** Make the label and switch one tap target and one accessible control. On and off need visibly different thumb positions and a non-color cue. Inline dependencies should stay next to the setting they depend on. Keep a visible explanation when a system permission prevents delivery.
- **Hierarchy and type.** Reuse the app’s 20 dp screen inset and 24 dp section spacing. Give the active duration the largest numerals on Timer and the selected period’s total the largest numerals on History. Give Log its stable input positions. A container earns its border or fill when its contents share a choice or action.
- **Large text.** The older timer capture splits History and Settings across lines in bottom navigation. The later native timer capture keeps all four names intact, and the current navigation code measures labels and accommodates wider ones. Retain a screenshot check for each appearance, a wide translation and enlarged type. Read the clock, choose a tab and change a switch using TalkBack on a device.

## Research behind the decisions

| Evidence | How it applies here |
| --- | --- |
| Oura’s published app guide describes a Today view that changes with the day and routes into historical data. | The current fast should govern what earns space on Timer. Longitudinal questions belong with dated records in History. [Oura app guide](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058599753-How-to-Use-the-Oura-App) |
| BodyFast’s help guide shows how someone selects a particular fasting period and adjusts its start or end. | When a user shifts dinner, carry the selected date through the edit and preview the revised clock times. That gives the planner an interaction specific to this product. [BodyFast plan editing](https://help.bodyfast.app/hc/en-001/articles/34652209248914-How-do-I-adjust-my-fasting-plan) |
| Android’s Settings guidance describes grouped subscreens, clear language and contextual access to frequent actions. | Keep recurring weekly edits available from the planner. Group occasional preferences by purpose. Use current values where they help someone choose the right row. [Android Settings patterns](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/patterns/settings) |
| Mobbin’s public illustrated sheet guide shows short contextual interactions and identifies where they become too complex. | Changing the timer readout or choosing an appearance fits in one sheet. Debugging reminder delivery needs room for status, explanation and a route to Android settings. [Mobbin bottom sheets](https://mobbin.com/glossary/bottom-sheet) |
| Purrweb’s Dribbble portfolio concept gives its clock and journal distinct compositions connected by one palette. The author presents a design study. | Use separate compositions for a clock, a dated history and an entry form. Retain the existing type, colors and mark across all three. The concept offers visual inspiration. Product behavior and commercial demand require direct testing. [Purrweb fasting tracker concept](https://dribbble.com/shots/22392705-Intermittent-Fasting-Tracker-Mobile-iOS-App) |

## Release evidence

Capture the same records, clock, viewport, theme and font scale before and after each layout change. Include empty and busy Log, a week with two identical fast lengths, a blocked reminder and larger text. For the pilot, record the first wrong tap, whether someone corrected it unaided and whether the data after reopening matched their expectation. For the paid review, record whether they can name a specific decision the four-week view helped them make. Willingness to pay requires a separate observed choice.
